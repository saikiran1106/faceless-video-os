For every reel/video, **write the full script/story and show it to the owner for approval FIRST**, before generating any keyframes, images, or Veo clips. the owner reads it, makes sure he understands the story, and asks for changes. Only after he approves the script do we move to production. Then audit **every subsequent step** the same way — show the output, get approval, then proceed to the next step.

**Why:** On the Shukracharya–Kacha reel, the visuals and voice-sync came out good but the STORY was half-baked and hard to understand (no setup, too much saga, characters didn't fit). Because we jumped straight to building, the weak story only surfaced after ~₹2,800 of generation. Catching story problems at the script stage is free; catching them after generation is expensive and demoralizing. the owner wants to steer the story before money is spent.

**How to apply:**
1. When the owner asks for a reel/video, first deliver ONLY the script/story (use [[reel-storywriting-playbook]] — hook, real setup, one clear idea, ≤2 characters, understandability test; consider part-wise serialization).
2. Present it plainly for him to read; explicitly invite changes. Do NOT generate anything yet.
3. Iterate on the script until he says take it forward.
4. After approval, proceed step-by-step (character sheets → keyframes → clips → compose), showing each step for approval before the next — this is a step-wise audit, not a fire-and-forget build.
5. Treat this as a hard gate: no image/video generation before an approved script.
