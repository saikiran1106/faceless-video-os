## The challenge
Imagine you are dropped alone on the African savanna at sunset.
The grass is tall. The light is going.
No phone, no torch, no weapon, and eleven hours until sunrise.
Tonight lions, spotted hyenas, a leopard and a two-ton hippo share this plain with you.
But the first danger is none of them.
Could you survive until morning?
## The heat
At six in the evening the ground still holds the heat of the day.
